# Boiler Feed Pump 101 Operating Procedure

Boiler Feed Pump 101 is a high-pressure pump used to supply boiler feedwater. Routine checks include vibration level, bearing temperature, and lubricant condition.

- Inspect bearing housings every shift.
- Verify pump alignment after any mechanical intervention.
- Confirm suction pressure is within operating range before startup.

If high vibration alarms occur, inspect the couplings, rotate the pump slowly, and check for fouling in the suction filter.
